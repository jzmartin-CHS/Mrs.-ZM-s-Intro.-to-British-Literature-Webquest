🌐 Journey Through British Literature

12th Grade ELA • 20-Minute Small Group WebQuest

WebQuest Task Instructions

Locate your assigned group station below and explore the provided trusted digital archives to answer these 3 core questions on your group paper:

  1. Historical Context: What major historical event, innovation, or conflict shaped this era's worldview?
  2. Key Authors & Works: Name at least two landmark authors and their famous works from this era.
  3. Literary Themes: What is one central theme, writing style, or values system dominant during this period?
Group 1

Old & Middle English (450–1485)

Anglo-Saxon warrior culture, oral storytelling traditions, medieval chivalry, and social satire.

  • Britannica: Anglo-Saxon History
  • British Library: Medieval Texts
  • History.com: Middle Ages Overview
Group 2

Renaissance & Early Modern (1485–1660)

The rebirth of art and humanism, the printing press, Elizabethan drama, and poetic sonnets.

  • Britannica: Renaissance Lit
  • British Library: Shakespeare Era
  • History.com: Tudor England
Group 3

Neoclassicism to Romanticism (1660–1837)

Enlightenment logic and reason shifting into nature worship, raw emotion, and Gothic mystery.

  • Britannica: Romantic Movement
  • British Library: Romantics & Gothic
  • History.com: Age of Reason
Group 4

The Victorian Era (1837–1901)

The Industrial Revolution, imperial expansion, social realism novels, and moral dilemmas.

  • Britannica: Victorian Literature
  • British Library: Victorian Writers
  • History.com: Industrial Era
Group 5

Modern & Postmodern Era (1901–Present)

WWI disillusionment, stream-of-consciousness writing, existentialism, and modern global voices.

  • Britannica: Literary Modernism
  • British Library: 20th-Century Lit
  • History.com: World War I Impact
